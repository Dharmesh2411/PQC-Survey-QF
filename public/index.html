WHY THIS MATTERS

Quantum risk is a transition to prepare for—not a deadline to guess at.

Understand the exposure, bring the right teams together, and make a practical plan.

01 · CRYPTOGRAPHY

Public-key protection will need to evolve

Cryptographically relevant quantum computers could undermine widely used public-key algorithms. Organizations need time to discover dependencies and plan changes.

02 · DATA LIFETIME

Harvest now, decrypt later

Encrypted information collected today may be valuable long after it is intercepted. Long-lived sensitive data makes preparation more urgent.

03 · MIGRATION

Readiness takes years

Inventory, supplier coordination, testing, and system upgrades take time. A clear baseline is a useful first step.
