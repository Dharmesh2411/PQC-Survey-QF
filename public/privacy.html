PRIVACY INFORMATION

Quadrafort's Quantum Risk Readiness Survey

This page explains how information is handled when you complete the survey. For Quadrafort's broader privacy information, see the Quadrafort privacy notice.

Information collected

Your responses to the survey questions are collected to calculate a readiness score and prepare an aggregate view of quantum-risk awareness, governance, cryptographic visibility, and migration readiness. If you choose to provide them, we also collect your name, work email, organization, designation, and phone number. We record your explicit consent and its timestamp, the browser user-agent, campaign parameters included in the survey link, and a keyed hash of the network address for abuse prevention. We do not store a raw IP address.

How information is used

Survey answers are used to calculate your score on the server, show your results, understand anonymized or aggregated readiness trends, and—only if you ask for it—send you a results report. If contact information is provided, it may be used to respond to a request for information about Quadrafort's post-quantum cryptography services.

Consent

The consent checkbox is unticked by default and is optional; you may submit your responses without ticking it. Your contact details are optional. Your consent choice is recorded along with its timestamp. You can request access to or deletion of your identifiable response using the contact details below.

Retention

Identifiable survey responses are retained for up to 730 days by default, after which an operator should delete them through the documented retention process. The configured retention duration may be changed by the survey operator. Backups have a separate, time-limited retention schedule.

Access and deletion requests

To request access to or deletion of your survey response, email info@quadrafort.com and include the work email address you used, if any, and the approximate submission date. An administrator will verify the request and process it using the protected administration area. Deletion is recorded in an audit log without retaining the response contents.

Security and hosting

Responses are stored in a PostgreSQL database operated by Quadrafort or its hosting provider. Production hosting and database location should be selected and configured by the operator in India, with HTTPS, restricted access, encrypted backups, and appropriate security controls.
